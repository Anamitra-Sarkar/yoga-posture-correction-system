\section{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:setup}
\runin{Data.} The cue-verified video corpus of Section~\ref{sec:method} supplies the training frames and the temporal windows (60 frames, stride 12; 8{,}002 windows; three folds split by video, so a model is tested on people and rooms it never saw). Photos supply variety: 6{,}616 photos from two public datasets (2{,}634 labelled with our poses and 3{,}982 of other poses, used as negatives), of which 4{,}931 are used for training and 1{,}685 are held out (1{,}037 of them other poses); and 422 freely licensed Wikimedia Commons photos with a frozen test split of 103, scored out-of-fold by models trained without the photo's fold. The held-out public photos share their sources with the training photos; the Commons photos come from another source and are called \emph{wild}. The rule engine covers 19 poses; the app offers eight guided target poses (Warrior II, Tree, Triangle, Chair, Downward Dog, Plank, Seated Easy, Corpse), and the temporal network recognises these eight plus transition/unknown.

\runin{Protocol and metrics.} A number counts only if it was measured on data the model never trained on. A pose \emph{passes} when its recall and precision are both at least 0.70 on at least 10 held-out photos (5 on the 103-photo set; 20 windows for the temporal network). A \emph{false alarm} is a photo of a pose that is not in the vocabulary that the system names as one of its poses (lower is better). We also report overall accuracy and macro recall. Validation accuracy printed by a trainer on a random frame split is never used as a result.

\runin{Baselines.} Five standard classifiers are trained on exactly the rows the gate model was trained on (136{,}506 rows of 15 angles) and scored with the same code: $k$-NN ($k{=}15$, distance-weighted), an RBF SVM, logistic regression, a 300-tree random forest and a plain MLP (256--256--128, ReLU, no residual blocks or batch normalisation). The rule engine alone is the non-learned baseline. Every run uses one random seed. For the temporal network we compare with an LSTM, a BiLSTM with multi-head attention, a temporal CNN, an MLP on window statistics and an ST-GCN without the skeleton graph, all with one recipe (AdamW \cite{loshchilov2019adamw}, last epoch scored).

\runin{Hardware and measurement.} Server compute is timed in-process on the 2-vCPU CPU host; request latency is the round trip of 32 sequential requests from a residential connection in India; phone throughput is measured on a POCO M7 Pro 5G (MediaTek Dimensity 7025, Android 16, Chrome 154) over a USB debugging connection with the app running in its real configuration. For the live interface a credited Wikimedia photograph stands in for the camera, so no screenshot shows a live person.
